## Remarks [#remarks]

The returned delegate is typically passed to `Win2DCanvas(...)` from [`Factories`](/microsoft-ui-reactor/main/reference/factories). If the delegate is consumed by animated callbacks, follow [the Win2D canvas threading guide](/microsoft-ui-reactor/main/controls/win2d-canvas#threading).
